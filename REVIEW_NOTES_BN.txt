SOURCE CHECKS:
- Bathroom entry: Sahih al-Bukhari 142
- Bathroom exit: Sunan Abi Dawud 30 (reference retained from Stage 1)
- Leaving home: Sunan Abi Dawud 5095 (Al-Albani: Sahih; other print editions may differ in grading)

CORRECTIONS IN THIS REVISION:
- English transliterations now distinguish long vowels, Arabic ʿayn, hamza, and emphatic sounds where practical.
- Mosque-entry pronunciation adjusted to include initial vowel (iftaḥ) in English, Bangla, Korean, Hindi and Indonesian.
- Improved several Korean, Hindi, Uzbek and Urdu reading aids.
- All 7 entries have 8 language keys in each of titles, pronunciations and meanings (automated test).

LIMITATIONS / PUBLICATION GATE:
- Romanized/local-script pronunciation is inherently approximate; Korean/Hindi/Bangla phonology cannot encode every Arabic consonant precisely.
- No qualified native speaker of each of the eight languages has independently signed off on every phrase; do NOT describe this as certified final linguistic review.
- Qur'anic Arabic recitation should be learned from qualified reciters, not transliteration alone.
- Flutter analyzer, Codemagic build, Android device UI and actual audio recitation were NOT executed here.
- Preserve existing repository backup and build/test on a debug branch before publishing.
